# Code of conduct

RoleSave follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about
the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing other people's private
information are not tolerated anywhere the project meets, including issues, pull
requests, and discussions.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through
their GitHub profile, [JainShreya26](https://github.com/JainShreya26). Reports
are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock
discussions, or block people who break this code of conduct, following the
enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
